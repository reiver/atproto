AT-Protocol (ATProto)

the decentralized social media protocol behind Bluesky, Blacksky, and others

by Charles Iliya Krempeaux

AT-Protocol (ATProto) is a open social web protocol. The “AT” in AT-Protocol (and ATProto) is short for Authenticated Transfer. So, AT-Protocol (and ATProto) means: Authenticated Transfer Protocol.

(ATProto is comparable to the ActivityPub open social web protocol.)

ATProto is used by Blacksky, Bluesky, Eurosky, Leaflet, Tangled, and others.

People that use ATProto have two types of identity.

Identity

The first type of identity that people that use ATProto have, is that they are identified by ‘@’ prefixed Internet domains. For example:

  • @example.com
  • @pfrazee.com
  • @reiver.bsky.social
  • @rude1.blacksky.team

The second type of identity that people that use ATProto have, is that they are identified by a decentralized identifier (DID). For example:

  • did:web:example.com
  • did:plc:ragtjsm2j2vknwkz3zp4oxrd
  • did:plc:scewmn2pl3oz36mxme2b6czz
  • did:plc:w4xbfzo7kqfes5zb7r6qv3rw

DID Methods

At the time of writing, two DID methods are well supported on the ATmosphere:

  • did:plc
  • Internet domain based did:web

There exists desire to add support for additional DID methods:

  • did:key
  • did:onion
  • path based did:web
  • various "web3" DIDs — did:ethr, did:ion, etc
